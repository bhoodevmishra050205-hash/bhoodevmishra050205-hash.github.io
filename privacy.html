How HHM Analytics uses your data

HHM Analytics is a local desktop component of HHM Shorts Automation for channel owners. This policy covers its Google authorization, YouTube Analytics sync and retention-guided editing features.

Data accessed

With your authorization, the app accesses your selected channel's name and identifier, video identifiers and metadata such as duration, view counts, and aggregate audience-retention reports. The read-only permissions are youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly. These reports do not identify individual viewers.

The app also processes OAuth access and refresh tokens, authorization status, sync timestamps and diagnostic information. It can use retention CSV files and observations you enter manually.

Purpose and use

The app verifies the connected channel, downloads available reports, identifies observed retention drops and suggests editing experiments for future two-part and three-part Shorts. Observations are not proof of why viewers left. This component does not use its read-only permissions to upload, modify or delete videos.

Local storage and retention

Tokens, channel reports, status and diagnostic files are stored as local JSON files under config/youtube_analytics/ in the app workspace. Retention observations are stored in config/v23_retention_feedback.json. Local project or run files may also contain the editing suggestions used for a particular Short.

These local files are not encrypted by the app. Access depends on your computer's account permissions, disk protection and backups. Raw reports and credentials remain until removed locally; the current observations store keeps up to 200 observations, while derived suggestions may remain in saved project files.

Sharing and AI integrations

Google receives authorization and API requests needed to provide the connection and reports. General editing suggestions derived from retention observations, their source label and a sample count can be passed to your configured GPT or ChatGPT integration when generating narration. The retention-feedback prompt excludes raw report rows, channel and video identifiers, and OAuth tokens.

The surrounding Shorts workflow may send source transcripts, story context and narration instructions through the configured AI integration. Processing by that service is subject to its applicable terms and privacy settings. This tool does not itself train AI models, sell Google user data or use it for advertising.

Google API data use

HHM Analytics' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Disconnecting and deleting data

You can revoke the app's Google access through your Google Account connections. Revocation stops further authorized access but does not remove reports already saved on your computer.

To erase local Analytics data, stop the app and remove its Analytics credentials, reports, status, diagnostics and retained observations from the local locations described above. Remove corresponding saved project files and backups if you also want their retained editing suggestions deleted. Deleting only local credentials does not revoke Google's authorization. Data already processed by an AI provider is governed by that provider's deletion controls.
