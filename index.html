HHM Analytics is a local desktop component of HHM Shorts Automation. It reads retention reports for your connected YouTube channels and uses those observations to suggest clearer openings, transitions and explanations in future Shorts.

How it works

  1. Choose a registered channel and authorize access with Google.
  2. The app checks that the selected channel matches the authorized channel.
  3. Eligible video metadata, view counts and aggregate audience-retention reports are saved locally.
  4. Retention observations inform editing suggestions for future two-part and three-part Shorts. They do not guarantee more views.

Why Google access is needed

YouTube read-only access identifies your channel and reads video information. YouTube Analytics read-only access retrieves aggregate viewing and retention reports. This Analytics component does not upload, edit or delete your videos.

Google authorization happens in your browser. The desktop app does not ask for your Google password.

Your data and AI-assisted editing

Channel reports and authorization tokens are stored on the computer running the app. General editing suggestions derived from retention observations can be included in prompts sent through your configured GPT or ChatGPT integration. Google authorization tokens and raw retention reports are not included in those narration prompts.
